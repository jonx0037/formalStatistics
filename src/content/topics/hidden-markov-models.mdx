abstract: >-
  Hidden Markov Models are the canonical latent-state probability model — a discrete Markov chain over hidden states emits observations through a state-conditional distribution. This topic develops the three classical HMM problems: likelihood evaluation (forward algorithm), state decoding (Viterbi), and parameter learning (Baum-Welch / EM). Continuous-time HMMs (Markov-modulated processes) and bioinformatics applications close out the treatment. Framed deliberately as "foundations of state-space inference" — the broader time-series content (Kalman filtering, ARIMA, GARCH) is reserved for future T9 topics that build on this foundation.
