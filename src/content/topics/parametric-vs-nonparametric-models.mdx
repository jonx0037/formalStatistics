abstract: >-
  The parametric/nonparametric distinction governs nearly every modeling decision in statistics: parametric models have a finite-dimensional parameter space (fixed regardless of sample size); nonparametric models have an infinite-dimensional parameter space or one that grows with the data. This topic formalizes the distinction (with semi-parametric as the bridge), surveys identifiability and consistency considerations, and develops the bias-variance trade-off as the operational consequence. Examples span Gaussian (parametric), kernel-density estimation (nonparametric), and Dirichlet processes (nonparametric Bayes).
