abstract: >-
  Empirical Bayes (EB) sits at the boundary between full Bayesian inference and frequentist shrinkage estimation. Type I EB plugs in a point estimate of the prior obtained from marginal-likelihood MLE; Type II EB estimates the mixing distribution itself. The James-Stein estimator is the canonical EB shrinkage rule. Robbins' compound-decision framework unifies the approach. Modern applications include variance-component estimation, mixed-effects models, and large-scale FDR (Efron & Tibshirani). This topic sits between Topic 28 (Hierarchical & Empirical Bayes) — which introduces EB as a boundary case — and the modern shrinkage literature that this topic develops in full.
